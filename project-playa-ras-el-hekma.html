Playa Ras El Hekma

Modern Coastal Living

Project Overview

Set against the backdrop of the breathtaking Ras El Hekma Bay, this 240-acre destination offers a luxurious Mediterranean-inspired escape. The project is meticulously planned to combine relaxation with active living, featuring extensive lush promenades, dedicated zones for cycling and skating, and an integrated commercial and cultural center. It is an ideal coastal haven for families looking to enjoy uninterrupted seaside tranquility.
