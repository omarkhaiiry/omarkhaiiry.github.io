Seashell Ras El Hekma

Exclusive North Coast Beachfront Resort

Project Overview

Seashell Ras El Hekma redefines coastal getaways on Egypt’s North Coast. Anchored by a stunning 1.5-kilometer beachfront and vast swimmable lagoons, this resort is designed to provide an immersive, nature-driven vacation experience. The property combines upscale beachfront homes with premium lifestyle amenities, making it a highly desirable summer retreat.

Key Features

  • 1.5km Private Beach
  • Seashore Swimmable Lagoons
  • G Residence (Cabanas, Lofts, Duplexes)
  • 7-Star Hotel Mondrian Hotel
  • Commercial District & Cultural Hub
  • Jogging & Cycling Trails
  • Sports club
