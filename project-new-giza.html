New Giza

Luxury Living West of Cairo

Project Overview

Spanning an impressive 1,500 acres, New Giza represents a massive leap in master-planned living within the 6th of October area. Situated conveniently along the Cairo-Alexandria Road, this expansive development places a massive premium on nature, dedicating nearly 90% of its footprint to parks, broad avenues, and landscaping. Designed around ten distinct, highly walkable neighborhoods, it provides residents with everything from top-tier educational facilities to a luxury 5-star hotel, golf courses, and vibrant dining experiences—all within a self-sustaining environment.
